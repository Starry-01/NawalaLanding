WHO WE ARE

More Than Music.

Marching band brings together musicians and performers who learn to move, play, and perform as one team.

Through regular practice and performances, members develop discipline, confidence, teamwork, and leadership.

WHAT WE DO

Train. Perform. Improve.

Every performance starts with practice, discipline, and teamwork.

01

Music Practice

Develop musical skills, timing, rhythm, and ensemble performance.

02

Marching Practice

Build coordination, formations, movement, and discipline.

03

Choreography

Combine music and movement into coordinated performances.

04

Competitions

Prepare together for competitions and major performances.

05

School Events

Perform and represent the school during important events and celebrations.

THE BAND

Find Your Section.

Every section has a different role, but every part works together as one band.

01

Brass

Trumpet, trombone, mellophone, and other brass instruments.

02

03

Visual performance, choreography, flags, and movement.

04

Leadership, conducting, coordination, and performance direction.
